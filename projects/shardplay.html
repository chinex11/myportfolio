The product

Shardplay is an arcade of 12 games, each with its own difficulty setting from 1 to 55. Players pay per round, the payment is verified on-chain, and every round played earns points toward a global leaderboard and per-game high scores.

Tic-Tac-Toe, Connect Four and Chess are live turn-based player-vs-player games. The other nine are invite-code score duels.

What I built

  • Pay-per-play payments: ETH payments checked against the real transaction on-chain for each round, with an admin-managed chain list.
  • Accounts: sign-in with Google, GitHub and X (OAuth 2.0, with PKCE for X), plus transactional email.
  • Superadmin panel: an integrations panel to manage chains and settings without touching code.
  • Public pages: home, About, Contact and a Roadmap that separates shipped features from planned ones.
  • Production setup: HTTPS configured through AWS Certificate Manager on a custom domain.

The design

An arcade-cabinet look on a near-black background, with a purple-to-teal-to-green gradient on the key headline words and a faceted gem logo. Calls to action use clipped-corner buttons, and monospace labels give the interface a game-console feel.
